## 4. Admission Process

### 4.1 Standard Admission Procedure

1. Create an applicant account on the GIT admissions portal.
2. Select the preferred academic program.
3. Complete personal, academic, and contact information.
4. Upload the required documents.
5. Enter qualifying entrance-examination details, where applicable.
6. Pay the non-refundable application fee.
7. Attend an entrance assessment, counselling session, or interview if required.
8. Review the provisional admission decision.
9. Accept the offer within the stated deadline.
10. Pay the admission confirmation fee.
11. Attend physical or online document verification.
12. Pay the remaining first-semester fees before enrolment.

### 4.2 Application Fees

- **Undergraduate application:** INR 1,000
- **Postgraduate application:** INR 1,500
- **International applicant processing fee:** INR 4,000
- Application fees are non-refundable and cannot be adjusted against tuition fees.

### 4.3 Required Documents

- Recent passport-size photograph
- Government-issued identity proof
- Class 10 mark sheet and certificate
- Class 12 mark sheet and certificate
- Bachelor's degree records for postgraduate applicants
- Entrance-examination scorecard, where applicable
- Transfer certificate
- Migration certificate, where applicable
- Community or category certificate, where applicable
- Income certificate for need-based scholarship applications
- Disability certificate for accessibility support, where applicable
- Passport and valid visa for international students

### 4.4 Document Verification

Original documents must be presented during verification. If an applicant cannot provide a required original document, the Admissions Officer may grant a conditional extension of up to 15 calendar days. Admission may be cancelled when documents are materially false, altered, or not submitted within an approved extension.

## 6. Scholarships and Financial Assistance

### 6.1 Academic Merit Scholarship

- Awarded to students with strong entrance and qualifying-examination performance.
- **Tier A:** 50% tuition waiver for students in the top 2% of the approved merit list.
- **Tier B:** 25% tuition waiver for students in the next 5% of the approved merit list.
- Renewal requires a minimum annual CGPA of 8.5, at least 85% attendance, and no disciplinary penalty.

### 6.2 Need-Based Support Grant

- Available to students whose annual family income does not exceed INR 500,000.
- Provides financial support of up to INR 60,000 per academic year.
- Applicants must submit an income certificate, bank details, and a personal statement.
- Continued support requires satisfactory academic progress and annual income verification.

### 6.3 Sports Excellence Scholarship

- Available to students who have represented a state or country in a recognized sport during the previous three years.
- Benefits range from a 20% to 75% tuition waiver.
- Selection includes certificate verification, a fitness assessment, and review by the Sports Scholarship Committee.
- Recipients must represent the institute when selected for eligible competitions.

### 6.4 Women in Technology Scholarship

- Available to eligible students admitted to B.Tech Computer Science, B.Tech Electronics, BCA, or MCA.
- Provides a tuition waiver of INR 35,000 per year.
- Selection considers academic merit and household income.
- Renewal requires a minimum CGPA of 7.5 and no active disciplinary case.

### 6.5 Scholarship Rules

A student may receive only one institute-funded tuition scholarship at a time. Government grants may be combined with an institute scholarship when the external scheme permits it. Scholarship applications must be submitted by 10 July 2026 unless a published scheme states a different date.

## 9. Academic Regulations

### 9.1 Credit and Grading System

Programs follow a semester-based Choice-Based Credit System. The institute uses a 10-point grading scale.

| Letter grade | Grade point | Performance description |
|---|---:|---|
| O | 10 | Outstanding |
| A+ | 9 | Excellent |
| A | 8 | Very good |
| B+ | 7 | Good |
| B | 6 | Above average |
| C | 5 | Satisfactory |
| P | 4 | Pass |
| F | 0 | Fail |

### 9.2 Attendance Requirements

- Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each registered course.
- Students with attendance from 65% through 74% may apply for condonation when absence is supported by medical, sports, family-emergency, or officially approved activity records.
- Students below 65% attendance are ordinarily not eligible to take the end-semester examination for that course.
- Attendance records are published monthly in the student portal.
- Students must report an attendance discrepancy within seven calendar days of publication.

### 9.3 Internal Assessment

For most theory courses, the final result includes 40% continuous internal assessment and 60% end-semester examination. Internal assessment may include quizzes, assignments, laboratory work, presentations, projects, and the mid-semester examination.

### 9.4 Academic Progress

- A minimum grade point of 4 is required to pass a course.
- Students must achieve a semester GPA of at least 5.0 to remain in good academic standing.
- A student with an SGPA below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters may be placed on academic probation.
- Students on probation must attend academic counselling and follow an approved improvement plan.

## 10. Examination Procedures

### 10.1 Examination Eligibility

A student must complete examination registration, pay applicable fees, satisfy attendance requirements, and clear administrative holds before receiving a hall ticket.

### 10.2 Examination Conduct

Students must arrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled start time. Student identity cards and hall tickets are mandatory. Mobile phones, smart watches, unauthorized notes, and communication devices are prohibited in the examination hall.

### 10.3 Absence from an Examination

A student who misses an examination due to a documented medical emergency, family bereavement, or officially approved representation may apply for a special examination within five working days. Approval is not automatic and requires review by the Examination Committee.

### 10.4 Revaluation

- Revaluation applications must be submitted within seven calendar days of result publication.
- The revaluation fee is INR 1,000 per course.
- A scanned answer-book copy may be requested for INR 500 per course.
- The revised result replaces the original result, whether the mark increases or decreases.

### 10.5 Supplementary Examination

Students who fail an eligible course may register for the next supplementary examination. A maximum passing grade of B may be awarded through a supplementary attempt unless the academic regulation for the program states otherwise.

## 16. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a commerce student apply for B.Tech Computer Science?

The applicant must satisfy the subject requirements for B.Tech Computer Science, including Physics and Mathematics. A commerce qualification without those subjects does not meet the standard eligibility requirement.

### Can scholarship benefits be combined?

A student may receive only one institute-funded tuition scholarship at a time. An institute scholarship may be combined with a government grant if the government scheme permits combination.

### What happens if attendance is 70%?

A student with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation with acceptable supporting evidence. Approval is not automatic.

### Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?

No. Hostel rooms depend on availability. Priority is given according to the published allocation criteria.

### Which office handles a result revaluation request?

The Examination Cell handles answer-book copy and revaluation requests.

### What should a student do after losing an identity card?

The student should report the loss to the Student Help Desk and Campus Security, submit a replacement request, and pay the applicable replacement charge.

### Are placement results guaranteed?

No. The institute provides preparation and recruitment opportunities, but an offer depends on employer requirements and student performance.
